# Kayuh — Full feature list

Agreed 21 Oct 2026. Phase numbers refer to [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md).

## 1. Map and discovery
- Colour-coded network: PCN / cycling path / shared path / footpath / road. Tap a segment to see why it got that tier. (P1)
- Layers: legal bike parking (sheltered or not), MRT stations, water points, toilets, hawker centres, bike shops, photo spots and viewpoints. (P1–P4)
- Named PCN loops and classic rides (Round Island Route, Coast-to-Coast, Rail Corridor, Southern Ridges) as one-tap guided routes. (P4)
- "Surprise me": a random nice route from your current location. (P4)

## 2. Planning
- Four modes: **A to B**, **Loop** (target distance or time), **Exploration** (chain loops and food stops), **Freeform** (tap and drag). (P2/P4)
- OneMap place and postal code search. (P2)
- Preference sliders: avoid roads, footpath tolerance, crossings. Re-routes instantly. (P2)
- Automatic stop ordering. (P4)
- Legal parking at the start, end and every stop, with the walk shown. (P2)
- Route alternatives side by side: nicest / balanced / most direct. (P2)
- Improve an existing route: import a GPX (Strava, RWGPS) and re-route it onto nicer paths. (P4)
- Route breakdown: distance, time at rental pace, % per tier, crossings, MRT bail-out points. (P2)

## 3. Rental pass legs
- Each rider sets an operator (HelloRide or Anywheel) and a per-ride cap (default 30 min). (P2)
- Routes are split into legs under the cap. Each leg ends at legal parking, preferably next to water, a toilet, food or shade. (P2)
- A group ride uses the strictest cap among its riders. (P5)
- Deep link to the HelloRide or Anywheel app to find a bike. (P2)

## 4. On the ride (phone, screen on)
- Follow mode: your position on the route, voice prompts, off-route warning, re-routing. (P5)
- Leg timer: "18 min left · parking in 1.2 km", with a nudge at 5 min left. (P5)
- MRT bail-out: nearest parking + station + the train home. (P5)
- High-contrast, big-button ride screen. (P5)

## 5. Watch
- "Send to watch": a GPX with parking and leg breaks as named waypoints. Import it into Huawei Health (GT 4) or Zepp (Amazfit). (P2)

## 6. Ride-day conditions
- NEA 2-hour rain forecast along the route; WBGT heat stress, lightning and haze (PSI) alerts. (P6)
- Sunset time and unlit-section warning. (P6)
- When raining: prefer sheltered parking and covered linkways. (P6)

## 7. Crews and group rides
- Crews joined by invite link. Shared route links work without an account. (P3)
- WhatsApp link previews: route image, distance, % PCN. (P3)
- Group rides: date and time, route, RSVPs, "meet in the middle", meeting point with legal parking, calendar invite. (P5)
- Reminders the evening before, plus a rain check on the morning of the ride (iOS web push from the home-screen app). (P5)

## 8. History and progress
- Rides **private by default**. Sharing with a crew is opt-in per ride, and the start and end are trimmed. (P5)
- Log a ride: "I rode this", GPX import (Huawei Health / Strava export), or a follow-mode recording. (P5)
- Personal heatmap, "% of PCN explored", a badge for each PCN loop finished. Combined crew map. (P5)

## 9. Data quality
- Mark a segment "great", "avoid" or "temporarily closed" (expires automatically). (P6)
- Monthly automatic data refresh, plus a report of OpenStreetMap fixes to make. (P1+)

## 10. App basics
- Offline once loaded, installs as a home-screen app, light and dark themes. (P1)
- Settings: operator, pass cap, riding speed, default preferences. (P2)
